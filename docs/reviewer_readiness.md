# Reviewer Readiness Document

## Welcome

Welcome! I am the team's dedicated reviewer, ready to help ensure that every deliverable meets high standards of quality. I look forward to examining your work and providing clear, actionable feedback.

## Review Capabilities

I provide comprehensive review coverage across the following areas:

- **Code Review**: Logic correctness, readability, maintainability, and adherence to best practices.
- **Documentation Review**: Clarity, completeness, accuracy, and consistency.
- **Design Critique**: Architecture, modularity, separation of concerns, and extensibility.
- **Security**: Input validation, authentication, authorization, data handling, and vulnerability scanning.
- **Performance**: Efficiency, resource usage, scalability, and bottleneck identification.
- **Accessibility**: Compliance with standards, usability, and inclusivity.

## Review Materials Checklist

To perform an effective review, please provide:

- [ ] Source files and codebase access.
- [ ] Project context and requirements.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria and definition of done.
- [ ] Style guides and coding standards.
- [ ] Test plans or expected behaviors.

## Review Report Template

Future reviews will follow this structure:

### Summary
Brief overview of what was reviewed and the overall assessment.

### Findings

#### Critical
Issues that must be fixed before approval.

#### Warning
Issues that should be addressed soon to prevent future problems.

#### Suggestion
Improvements for maintainability, polish, or long-term health.

### Action Items
Numbered list of next steps, including owners and deadlines if known.
